## Repetition and teaching limits

The original basic/business-context pairs in the five revenue families largely repeat one recognition decision. Clinic/workshop rent repeat current occupancy versus ownership/future benefit. Borrowing and share-issuance planned-payroll/planned-equipment variants repeat source versus intended use. Full prepayment in batch 10 overlaps batch 8's full morning receipt; its added cue is the explicit paid-in-full receipt label. Retain this as wording transfer, not a new recognition rule. Third-party service payment/collection form a deliberate contrast with the same payer cue and different prior recognition. Delayed invoice/certificate form a paperwork-versus-performance comparison, with different underlying recorded balances.

Other additions supply distinct prior balances, partial amounts, counterparty roles, consumption without production/claims, ownership of secondhand resources, and payment amount/timing distinctions. All remain within thirteen canonical families. Zero exact duplicates does not establish zero semantic repetition. No existing published scenario was silently rewritten or retired at this checkpoint.

Shared category/direction/side teaching appropriately repeats rules. Several inherited options remain weak or untagged (including filler payable options); full prepayment and third-party wording do not always have a dedicated distractor for every introduced cue. Amount interpretation is generally explained rather than tested through amount options. These limitations prevent treating the count as complete misconception coverage. Automatic matched-contrast scheduling and X4 offline explanation repetition remain deferred; no mastery/evidence logic or learner history changed.

All scenarios have three allowed amounts. The 52 additions use $50/$100/$200; older eighteen retain their published larger sets. Do not normalize these without versioned review. Supported course scope is still provisional because no local syllabus/slides were found; no interest, depreciation, inventory, accrual, or other new family was added.
