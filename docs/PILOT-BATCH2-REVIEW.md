The clinic pair uses a one-month policy to keep amounts small while making the unused/fully-used distinction explicit. It avoids a proration calculation or a new event family. Its rent contrast pays for current use rather than a resource still available for later months. The studio questions distinguish owners from lenders and suppliers; the share scenario deliberately retains the future-payroll cue from batch 1's borrowing scenario, requiring classification by the source of cash rather than its intended use.

Review refinements: the investment direction explanation avoids the borrowing family's irrelevant repayment language; prepaid/rent explanations address distractors actually offered at that stage. All scenarios explicitly state relevant timing, prior recording, cash movement, and remaining benefits or obligations. Account answers are not named in scenario text. Every template inherits complete reviewed teaching and overrides the event-specific stages; investment also overrides direction. Standard family distractors and snapshot routing remain applicable.
